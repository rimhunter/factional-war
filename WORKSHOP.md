[h1]Factional War (rimhunter)[/h1]

Two NPC factions fight a real battle that you can watch, join or loot. Works on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 from one subscription.

This is a continuation of Shadowrabbit's Factional War: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2534328163]Shadowrabbit's Workshop page[/url] and [url=https://github.com/Shadowrabbit/FactionalWar]Shadowrabbit's source on GitHub[/url]. It is built from Shadowrabbit's own source (the last commit, 2022-10-22). Where this version uses work from the 1.5 continuation by Ionfrigate12345 or the 1.6 fork by llunak, the changelog and the commit name the author.

[h2]What it does[/h2]

This mod adds 4 types of incidents. In these incidents, you are not the protagonist, and you are not at the center of the war. You are just a bystander, and you have the right to choose whether to be involved in the incident.

The enemy's AI behaves in more detail: it treats itself, kills wounded enemies, rescues wounded friends, destroys enemy factions and plunders loot, assaults enemy faction camps, destroys enemy rooms to find loot, and kidnaps members of hostile factions.

[h2]Known issues[/h2]

[list]
[*]On the large site maps you reach by caravan (about 50 pawns on each side), a battle sometimes stalls with both sides standing, or one side leaves after almost no fighting. The cause is not yet found.
[*]In every language except English, three new texts (the settings window title, its Default button and a Dev mode button) show in English until a translator adds them.
[/list]
