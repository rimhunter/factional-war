# Factional War (rimhunter)

A continuation of [Factional War](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2534328163) by Shadowrabbit, for RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7, by rimhunter. It is built from Shadowrabbit's own source, [Shadowrabbit/FactionalWar](https://github.com/Shadowrabbit/FactionalWar), as of its last commit (3d35b55, 2022-10-22). Ionfrigate12345's 1.5 continuation ([Ionfrigate12345/Rimworld_FactionalWarContinued](https://github.com/Ionfrigate12345/Rimworld_FactionalWarContinued)) and llunak's 1.6 fork ([llunak/rimworld-FactionalWar](https://github.com/llunak/rimworld-FactionalWar)) are credited where their work is used; each such fix is named in `CHANGELOG.md`. rimhunter claims no ownership of the original, and will hand the mod back or take it down at Shadowrabbit's request.

This mod adds 4 types of incidents. In these incidents you are not the protagonist and not at the center of the war: you are a bystander, and you choose whether to take part. The enemy's AI behaves in more detail: it treats itself, kills wounded enemies, rescues wounded friends, destroys enemy factions and plunders loot, assaults enemy faction camps, destroys enemy rooms to find loot and kidnaps members of hostile factions.

Do not run another Factional War build alongside this one. RimWorld's mod list warns you when the original, the 1.5 continuation or the 1.6 fork is enabled together with it.
