# Verification screenshots, RimWorld 1.7.5011

Taken windowed by the `screenshot-*` scenarios and the settings window scenario (`scripts/run-scenarios --windowed --save-screenshots DIR -s <name>`) on a 3840x2160 window, with the game's own renderer. Each full image is scaled to 1152 pixels wide by the runner; `-details` files are native-resolution crops of the same capture. Every scenario asserts what the game drew or placed (letter text, drawn labels, pawns of both sides on screen) before the image is kept. Pawn names are the game's generated names; no personal name, Steam name or file path is on screen (the game window is captured, not the desktop).

The two sides of a battle are not colored differently by the game, so they cannot be told apart by eye; each battle scenario requires at least one standing pawn of each side on screen and records how many were in the run's `results.json` metrics (`<shot>: <side> pawns on screen`).

## Known limits

- Vanilla log-error flakes. The runner (not these scenarios) fails a scenario on any `Log.Error` raised while it runs, and RimWorld itself logs errors in battles with only vanilla frames in the stack: `Tried to destroy already-destroyed thing Fire<id>`, `Spawning destroyed thing Gun_TripleRocket...` (with its two companion lines), `Job N faulted with exception: NullReferenceException` in `PawnUtility.PawnBlockedBy`, `Never haulable ActiveDropPod... blocking Blueprint_Turret_Mortar...`, and, with Anomaly, an `IndexOutOfRangeException` from `CompAbilityEffect_Burner`. Measured rate: about 1 failing execution in 10 of the battle scenarios on 1.7; on 1.6 the loot scenario failed 3 of 5 attempts on the Fire and TripleRocket errors.
- No full pass was clean in one run on either version; each full pass had one to two failures, and every scenario passed at least once.
- Other measured flakes (1.7): `player-side-harming-a-friendly-side...` failed in setup once in about 8 runs; `player-side-helping-a-side-with-an-allied-faction...` passed 1 of 3 in one sequence; battles on the 250x250 caravan site maps sometimes stall or end without a fight (about 6 of 16 early loot attempts, 3 of about 25 in-progress attempts).
- A follow-up in the shared runner (`rimhunter-testrunner`) is needed for an expected-error list.
- All 1.7 screenshots and counts are from RimWorld 1.7.5011. After the 1.6 round trip Steam delivered 1.7.5013; it was only spot-checked (2 headless and 2 windowed scenarios). Windowed launches on 5013 need `--game-env WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-0` until the shared runner passes it.
- The Empire, shambler and changed-settings shots exist only for 1.7 (the DLC scenarios and the click helper are 1.7-only; there is no `docs/verification/1.6` equivalent).
- The per-scenario Player.log read was removed from the battle scenarios on purpose: the runner's `Log.Error` check runs for every scenario, and the old read failed every later scenario in a launch for one earlier error. The cost is that Unity exceptions that bypass `Log.Error` are no longer caught in battle scenarios.
